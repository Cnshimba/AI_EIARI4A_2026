Choose Your Specialisation

Welcome to the final assessment of the AI Module. For this project, you have the flexibility to choose a path that aligns with your career interests. Whether you want to specialise in Cyber-Security AI (our core focus) or explore General AI Development, the choice is yours.

Note: You must select ONE of the projects below. All projects carry equal weight (40%) and are graded on the same rigorous engineering standards.

Common Deliverables

Regardless of which project you select, you must submit the following 3 items. Grading rigour is identical across all options.

1. Jupyter Notebook

  • End-to-End Code:
    Loading, Cleaning, Model, Eval.
  • Markdown:
    Detailed explanations for logic.

2. Technical Report

  • Format:
    PDF, 4-6 pages, IEEE/Academic.
  • Content:
    Methodology, Arch, Critical Thinking.

3. Presentation

  • Format:
    5-minute video or slide deck.
  • Focus:
    Business value & Results.
Common Learning Outcomes
By the end of this project, you will be able to:
1. Apply a full Deep Learning workflow (Data &to; Model &to; Insights).
2. Evaluate performance using domain-appropriate metrics.
3. Communicate results professionally to stakeholders.
