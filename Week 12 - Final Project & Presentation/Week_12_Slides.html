Week 12: Final Project Launch

Week 12

Final Capstone Project

Building an Intrusion Detection System

1. The Mission

You have learned Regression, Classification, CNNs, NLP, and Ethics. Now, put it all together.

Scenario

A corporate network is under attack. You have been given a raw PCAP (Packet Capture) file. Your job is to build an AI that can distinguish between normal employees and malicious hackers.

2. The Pipeline

You will build the full Cybersecurity Data Science pipeline:

  1. Data Ingestion: Raw PCAP (packets).
  2. Feature Extraction: Convert PCAP to CSV flows (using Zeek).
  3. Preprocessing: Cleaning, Normalizing, Encoding.
  4. Modeling: Training Deep Learning models (Supervised & Unsupervised).
  5. Evaluation: Detecting the attacks.

3. Techniques Required

This project is worth 40% of your grade. Use professional tools.

  • Zeek / CICFlowMeter: To turn packets into data.
  • Pandas: To clean the dirty data.
  • PyTorch: To build the Brain.
  • Autoencoders: To catch "Zero-Day" (unknown) attacks by learning what "Normal" looks like.

4. Deliverables

What you must submit

  1. Jupyter Notebook: Working code, graphs, and logic.
  2. Technical Report: 4-6 pages explaining *why* you made your design choices.

5. Timeline

  • Week 13: Data Extraction & Cleaning.
  • Week 14: Model Training.
  • Week 15: Evaluation & Report Writing.
  • Deadline: End of Semester.

6. Good Luck!

This is the bridge between being a Student and being an Engineer.
