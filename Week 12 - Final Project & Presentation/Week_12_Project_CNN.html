Option B: Image Classifier (CNN)

Project: Computer Vision Classifier

Goal: Build and optimize a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) to classify images into at least 5 distinct categories.

Specific Learning Outcomes
1. Implement Data Augmentation to solve overfitting.
2. Design deep ConvNet architectures (filters, pooling, dropout).
3. Visualize intermediate layer activations (Explainable AI).

1. The Scenario

Choose a real-world visual problem. Examples include: identifying crop diseases from leaf photos, classifying different types of traffic signs for autonomous systems, or detecting fractures in medical X-ray imaging. Your model must be robust enough to handle variations in the input data.

2. Tools & Constraints

  • Dataset: You must find a suitable dataset (Kaggle, Google Dataset Search). Minimum 5 classes, minimum 500 images per class.
  • Framework: TensorFlow (Keras) or PyTorch.
  • Constraint: You cannot use a pre-trained model (like ResNet50) as your primary submission. You must build a CNN from scratch first. You can compare against Transfer Learning for extra credit.

3. Step-by-Step Pipeline

Step 1: Data Preprocessing & Augmentation

Raw images are rarely perfect. You must implement specific augmentations relevant to your domain:

# Example Keras Augmentation
datagen = ImageDataGenerator(
    rotation_range=20,
    width_shift_range=0.2,
    horizontal_flip=True,
    rescale=1./255
)

Requirement: Visualize the augmented images in your notebook.

Step 2: Architecture Design

Build a CNN with at least:

  • 3 Convolutional Blocks (Conv2D + ReLU + MaxPooling).
  • Flatten Layer.
  • Dense (Fully Connected) Layers.
  • Dropout (to prevent overfitting).

Step 3: Training & Tuning

Monitor Training vs Validation Loss. If your model overfits (low training loss, high validation loss), you must adjust:

  • Dropout rate.
  • L2 Regularization.
  • Augmentation intensity.

Step 4: Explainability (Crucial)

DL models are black boxes. You must "open" them.

  • Visualizing Filters: Show what patterns the first Conv layer is detecting (edges, textures).
  • Activation Maps: Pass a test image and show the feature map output of the 2nd Convolutional layer.

Step 5: Evaluation

  • Confusion Matrix (Which classes get mixed up?).
  • Show the "Most Confused" images (Why did it fail?).
