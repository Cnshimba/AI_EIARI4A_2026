2. Week 9 Goal

Teaching Machines to Read

Until now, we've done math on numbers and images. Text is different—it's messy and discrete. This week, we introduce Natural Language Processing (NLP). We'll learn how to turn words into vectors (Embeddings) so our neural networks can understand human language.

3. The Language Gap

Computers don't speak English. They speak Matrix Multiplication.

  • The Problem: How do we feed "Hello" into a Neural Network?

  • The Old Way: ASCI/Unicode? (Too fragile).

  • The ML Way: Convert words to meaningful vector representations.

4. The NLP Pipeline

  1. Corpus: Your raw text (dataset).
  2. Tokenization: Splitting text into chunks (words/sub-words).
  3. Indexing: Assigning a unique ID to each token.
  4. Embedding: Converting IDs to dense vectors.

5. Tokenization

Splitting the string.

  • Word-Level: "I love AI" -> ["I", "love", "AI"]

  • Char-Level: "AI" -> ["A", "I"]

  • Sub-word (BPE): "Unbeatable" -> ["Un", "beat", "able"] (Modern Standard)

6. Embeddings vs One-Hot

One-Hot Encoding

  • Sparse, high-dimensional.

  • "Cat" = [0, 1, 0...], "Dog" = [0, 0, 1...]

  • Issue: No relationship between words. Dot product is 0.

Word Embeddings

  • Dense, lower-dimensional.

  • Learned representations where similar words allow similar vectors.

  • King - Man + Woman = Queen.

7. Checkpoint 1

Embedding Logic

If "Happy" has a vector [0.9, 0.1] and "Sad" has [-0.9, -0.1], what happens if we minimize the distance between "Good" and "Happy"?

Click for Answer "Good" will move closer to `[0.9, 0.1]`. The model learns that "Good" and "Happy" mean similar things because they appear in similar contexts.

8. EmbeddingBag (PyTorch)

A fast, efficient way to handle variable-length text.

self.embedding = nn.EmbeddingBag(
    num_embeddings=vocab_size, 
    embedding_dim=64, 
    sparse=False
)

  • What it does: Looks up vectors for all words in a sentence and Averages them into one single vector.

  • Why: Feeding variable length sentences into a Linear layer is hard. Averaging makes them fixed size!

9. Summary

  • NLP: Bridge between human text and machine math.

  • Tokenization: Chopping text into math-able pieces.

  • Embeddings: The secret sauce that gives words meaning (geometry).

  • EmbeddingBag: A simple, fast way to classify text by averaging word vectors.
