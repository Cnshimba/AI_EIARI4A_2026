Welcome to Week 2: Mathematics for AI!

Mathematics is not a barrier—it's the engine under the hood. This week, we demystify the equations you see in AI papers.
We adopt a "Math as Code" approach:
1. Concept: Understand the intuition (Geometric).
2. Formula: See the math equations.
3. Code: Implement it in Python.
4. Application: See exactly where it is used in modern AI (transformers, backprop, etc.).

1. Linear Algebra: The Structure of Data

1. Vectors (The Building Block)

1. The Concept (Intuition)
A vector is just an Arrow in space.

  • Direction: Where it points.

  • Magnitude: How long it is.

2. The Formula & Code
$$ \mathbf{x} = [x_1, x_2, \dots, x_n] $$

x = np.array([3, 2]) # Move 3 Right, 2 Up

3. AI Application
In AI, Everything is a Vector.

  • NLP: "King" = [0.91, 0.42, ...].

  • Recommender Systems: You = [LikesAction, LikesRomance].
    Finding similar movies is just finding vectors pointing in the same direction.

2. Matrices (The Transformation Machine)

1. The Concept (Intuition)
A Matrix is NOT just a spreadsheet.
It is a Function that warps space.
It can Rotate, Scale, or Shear the entire grid.

2. The Formula & Code
$$ \mathbf{Ax} = \mathbf{b} $$

A = np.array([[1, 1], [0, 1]]) # Shear Matrix
new_vec = A @ vec

3. AI Application
Neural Networks are chains of matrix transformations.
100 layers = 100 consecutive warps of the input image until the answer becomes clear.

3. The Dot Product

1. The Concept (Intuition)
Measuring Alignment.

  • Parallel arrows? High Score.

  • Perpendicular? Zero.

  • Opposite? Negative.

2. The Formula & Code
$$ \mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = \sum a_i b_i $$

score = np.dot(inputs, weights)

3. AI Application
This is the Attention Mechanism in Transformers (GPT).
It calculates similarity scores between your query ("Capital?") and the database ("Paris").

4. Eigenvectors (The Principal Axes)

1. The Concept (Intuition)
When a matrix spins the world, Eigenvectors are the axes that stay still. They define the shape of the data cloud.

2. The Formula
$$ \mathbf{A}\mathbf{v} = \lambda \mathbf{v} $$

3. AI Application
PCA (Principal Component Analysis).
We find these axes to compress data (dimensionality reduction). Keeping the top 2 eigenvectors captures 90% of the information with 10% of the size.

Section 1 Checkpoint

Summary

  • Vectors: Data points (Arrows).

  • Matrices: Functions that warp space.

  • Dot Product: Similarity score (Attention).

  • Eigenvectors: Principal axes (Compression).

2. Calculus: The Engine of Learning

1. The Gradient (The Compass)

1. The Concept (Intuition)
You are on a foggy mountain (Loss Function) at night.
You want to reach the valley (Minimum Error).
The Gradient tells you which way is Uphill. You walk Downhill.

2. The Formula & Code
$$ \theta_{new} = \theta_{old} - \alpha \nabla f $$

grad = calculate_slope(x)
x = x - learning_rate * grad

3. AI Application
Gradient Descent.
This is how ALL neural networks learn. They nudge weights downhill millions of times until they reach the bottom (Optimized Model).

2. The Chain Rule (The Ripple Effect)

1. The Concept (Intuition)
Networks are deep layers of functions: $f(g(h(x)))$.
If the output is wrong, who is to blame?
The Chain Rule ripples the blame backwards.

2. The Formula
$$ \frac{dz}{dx} = \frac{dz}{dy} \times \frac{dy}{dx} $$

3. AI Application
Backpropagation.
It allows us to train Deep networks. Without it, we couldn't assign error to the early layers of a deep, complex model.

3. Probability: The Logic of Uncertainty

Bayes' Theorem

1. The Concept (Intuition)
Updating your beliefs based on new evidence.
Prior Belief + New Evidence = Posterior Belief.

2. The Formula
$$ P(A|B) = \frac{P(B|A) P(A)}{P(B)} $$

3. AI Application
Robotics & Self-Driving Cars.
"I thought the road was clear (Prior), Lidar saw a blob (Evidence), now I believe it's a car (Posterior)."
