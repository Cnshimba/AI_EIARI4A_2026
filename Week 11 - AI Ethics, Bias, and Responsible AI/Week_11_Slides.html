2. Week 11 Goal

Responsible AI

We have built powerful models. Now we ask: Should we use them? AI systems affect real people—loans, hiring, policing. This week, we learn to audit our models for Bias, make them Explainable (XAI), and ensure they are Safe and Robust.

3. The Myth of Neutrality

"Math can't be racist." Wrong.

  • Models learn from Data.

  • Data is History.

  • History is Biased.

  • $\therefore$ Models automate History (and Bias).

[!TRUE]
If you train on resumes from the 1980s, your model will learn that "Men are Executives" and "Women are Secretaries."

4. Explainable AI (XAI)

The "Black Box" Problem: Deep Learning models are millions of numbers. We don't know why they decide things.

Techniques

  1. LIME/SHAP: Perturbs inputs to see what changes the output. "Use this to see which pixels made the car a car."
  2. Feature Importance: (Trees) Which columns matter most?

5. Checkpoint 1

Bias Audit

You train a facial recognition model. It works 99% of the time on White men but only 60% of the time on Black women.
Is this model "Accurate"?

Click for Answer **Mathematically? Maybe.** If your test set is 90% White men, the average accuracy is high. **Ethically? No.** It is biased and discriminatory. You must analyze accuracy **per subgroup**.

6. The AI Engineering Responsibility

It is no longer enough to just "fit()" and "predict()".

  • Audit Data: Check representation before training.

  • Audit Logic: Use XAI tools to verify reasoning.

  • Audit Outcomes: Check disparate impact on different groups.
